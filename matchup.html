📊 Rating System Explained (0-1000 Scale)

                
            

            
            

                

                    
📌 Custom Rating Formula: Uses lifetime statistics (Games, Wins, Win %) with experience-based reliability.

                


                

                    
Formula:

                    

                        
R₁₀₀₀ = K × R_E

                        
R_E = 1000 / (1 + e^(-10 × (P_W - 0.5)))

                        
K = 1 - e^(-G/250)

                    

                    
Where: G = Total Games, W = Wins, P_W = Win % (W/G)

                


                

                    
Component Breakdown:

                    
    
                        
  • R_E (Performance Score): Maps win % to 0-1000 using logistic curve
    • 
                        
  • K (Experience Factor): Reliability multiplier (0 to 1) based on games played
    • 
                        
  • 50% Win Rate → 500 points (neutral performance)
    • 
                        
  • 100% Win Rate → ~1000 points (maximum performance)
    • 
                    

                


                

                    
Experience Factor (K):

                    

                        
• 10 games: K = 0.04 (4% reliability)

                        
• 50 games: K = 0.18 (18% reliability)

                        
• 100 games: K = 0.33 (33% reliability)

                        
• 250 games: K = 0.63 (63% reliability)

                        
• 500 games: K = 0.86 (86% reliability)

                        
• 1000 games: K = 0.98 (98% reliability)

                    

                


                

                    
Examples:

                    

                        
🎲 Game A: 50W-50L (50% win rate, 100 games)

                        
R_E = 500, K = 0.33 → Rating = 165

                        

                        
🎲 Game B: 8W-2L (80% win rate, 10 games)

                        
R_E = 982, K = 0.04 → Rating = 39

                        

                        
🎲 Game C: 80W-20L (80% win rate, 100 games)

                        
R_E = 982, K = 0.33 → Rating = 324

                        

                        
High win % needs volume to reach top ratings!

                    

                


                

                    
Key Insights:

                    
    
                        
  • Win percentage drives performance score (R_E)
    • 
                        
  • Games played determines reliability (K factor)
    • 
                        
  • Need both high win % AND volume for top ratings
    • 
                        
  • Early wins are heavily discounted (small K)
    • 
                        
  • 500+ games needed for ~86% reliability
