📊 ELO Rating Explained

                
            

            
            

                

                    
📌 Note: BGG Rating uses the community average rating from BoardGameGeek (0-10 scale), not your personal rating.

                


                

                    
Formula:

                    

                        ELO = (W/T)³ × T × 100 × (1 + BGGAvgRating/10)
                    

                    
Where: W = Wins, T = Total Matchups, BGGAvgRating = BGG Community Average (0-10 scale)

                


                

                    
Component Breakdown:

                    
    
                        
  • Win Percentage (W/T)³: Cubed to heavily reward consistency & efficiency
    • 
                        
  • Total Matchups (T): More games played = higher ELO potential
    • 
                        
  • BGG Avg Rating: Community rating (affects multiplier 1.0-2.0x)
    • 
                        
  • Rating Multiplier: 1.0x (3.0 rating) to 2.0x (10.0 rating)
    • 
                    

                


                

                    
Examples:

                    

                        
🎲 Game A: 8W-2L (80% win rate), BGG Avg Rating 7.5

                        
ELO = (0.8)³ × 10 × 100 × (1 + 7.5/10)

                        
ELO = 0.512 × 10 × 100 × 1.75 = 896.0

                        

                        
🎲 Game B: 8W-2L (80% win rate), BGG Avg Rating 4.0

                        
ELO = (0.8)³ × 10 × 100 × (1 + 4.0/10)

                        
ELO = 0.512 × 10 × 100 × 1.4 = 716.8

                        

                        
Same win record, but Game A (7.5 rating) has 25% higher ELO due to higher quality game!

                    

                


                

                    
Key Insights:

                    
    
                        
  • Consistency matters most (win % cubed)
    • 
                        
  • Experience matters (more matchups = more score)
    • 
                        
  • Game quality matters (higher rated games reward more)
    • 
                        
  • Winning with 6.0+ rated games is roughly 1.6x better than 3.0 rated games
    • 
                    

                


                

                    
Why This Works:

                    
    
                        
  • 🎯 Rewards high win rates (cubing effect)
    • 
                        
  • 🔢 Encourages more plays (T multiplier)
    • 
                        
  • ⭐ Factors game quality (BGG rating bonus)
    • 
                        
  • 📈 Same record, better games = higher ELO
    •
