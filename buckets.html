About Bucket Ranking

This app helps you rank large lists of items by breaking them into manageable "buckets" first, then comparing items within those buckets.

How it works

  • 01. Add your items individually or in bulk.
  • 02. Distribute items into buckets based on general quality.
  • 03. Perform head-to-head comparisons within buckets.
  • 04. Review your precision-ranked list!

Comparison Modes

ROUND ROBIN

Every item vs every other item. Maximum accuracy, more time.

MERGE SORT

Efficient algorithm logic. Fewer comparisons, high precision.
