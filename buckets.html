About Bucket Ranking App

This app helps you rank items using a bucket-based comparison system. Perfect for ranking products, ideas, candidates, or anything else!

How It Works

  • Add items individually or bulk load them
  • Choose the number of buckets (2, 3, 4, 5, or 10)
  • Assign items to buckets one at a time
  • Compare items within each bucket
  • Get final rankings with detailed stats

Comparison Modes

Round Robin: Every item is compared with every other item in its bucket. More accurate but takes more comparisons.

Merge Sort: Uses an efficient sorting algorithm. Faster with fewer comparisons but may be less accurate for subjective rankings.

Features

  • Individual and bulk item entry
  • Flexible bucket configurations (2, 3, 4, 5, or 10)
  • Two comparison algorithms
  • Undo last comparison
  • View comparison history
  • Export results as JSON or text
  • Copy rankings to clipboard
  • Mobile-friendly responsive design

Tips

• Higher bucket numbers = better items (Bucket 10 > Bucket 1)

• Use fewer buckets for items that vary widely in rank

• Use more buckets for items that are more similar

• Round Robin is better for subjective rankings

• Merge Sort is faster for objective comparisons
