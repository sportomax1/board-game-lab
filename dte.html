Sort Options Explained

📋 Default

Logic: Sorts by shelf location (alphanumeric), then by name.

Use when: You want to browse games organized by their physical location.

📚 Shelf

Logic: Sorts alphabetically by shelf location.

Use when: You want to find games by shelf number or location.

🔤 Name

Logic: Sorts alphabetically by game name (A-Z).

Use when: You're looking for a specific game by name.

📅 Year

Logic: Sorts by publication year (newest first), then by BGG rating, then by name.

Use when: You want to find the newest games or browse by era.

⭐ Rating

Logic: Sorts by BGG community rating (highest first).

Use when: You want to find the highest-rated games according to BoardGameGeek users.

🏆 Rank

Logic: Sorts by BGG overall rank (best rank first).

Use when: You want to see the most popular/highly-ranked games on BGG.

⏱️ Playing Time

Logic: Sorts by playing time (shortest first), then by BGG rating (highest first), then by name (A-Z).

Use when: You want to find games that fit a specific time constraint or prefer shorter games.

❤️ Desire

Logic: Sorts by personal desire level (highest first), then by BGG rating, then by name.

Use when: You want to prioritize games you're most excited to play based on your wishlist and want-to-play status.

🎯 Smart Pick (Dynamic Score)

Advanced Multi-Factor Algorithm

📊 Formula Components:

  • • Desire Score (40%): Your personal interest level (0-10) × 4.0
  • • Quality Score (30%): BGG Rating (0-10) × 3.0
  • • Popularity Score (20%): Rank bonus (top 100: +2.0, top 1000: +1.0, top 10000: +0.5)
  • • Time Efficiency (10%): Playing time bonus (≤60min: +1.0, ≤120min: +0.5, ≤180min: +0.25)

🎲 Calculation:

score = (desire × 4.0) + (rating × 3.0) + rankBonus + timeBonus

Maximum possible score: 50 points

💡 Use Smart Pick when:

You want AI-powered recommendations that balance your personal interest with game quality, popularity, and time commitment. Perfect for finding the "best bang for your buck" games that match your preferences and schedule!
