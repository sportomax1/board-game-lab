About / How to play

Objective: Be the first player to reach the win score (default 21).

  • Each turn a player may roll up to 3 times.
  • After any roll the player may Keep that roll — it is added to their total score.
  • If a player uses all rolls they must keep the final roll.

Lobbies: create or join a lobby (1–4 players). The lobby host can start the game. Players can leave or delete the lobby.

Spectators: non-active players see the same dice area and counters but action buttons are disabled — they can watch the active player's rolls live. The client uses real-time updates plus a 1s polling fallback.
