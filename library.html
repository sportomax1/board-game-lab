📊 Desire Score Explained

What is Desire Score?

The Desire Score ranks games by how much you want to play them right now, combining multiple factors:

✅ Owned Games (Highest Weight)

Games you own score highest since you have them available

🎮 Want to Play

Games on your "Want to Play" list show intent to try them

⏪ Previously Owned

You've played these before and might want to revisit them

🎁 Want / Wishlist

Future potential games you're interested in

❌ Not Available

Games on other lists (For Trade, Want to Buy) score lower

Sorting Logic:

  1. Games you Own = Highest priority
  2. Games you Want to Play = High priority
  3. Previously Owned = Medium priority
  4. Games on your Wishlist = Lower priority
  5. Games in other collections = Lowest
