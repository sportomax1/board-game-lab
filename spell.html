About This App

BGG Game Speller is a fun word-guessing game using your BoardGameGeek collection!

  • Enter your BGG username to load your owned games
  • Watch as the spinner reveals letters one by one
  • See how many games match the current pattern
  • The game continues until only one possibility remains
  • Use Undo to go back or Restart to try again

Tip: Add this app to your home screen for quick access!
