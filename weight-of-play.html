What the numbers mean

The play-weighted average repeats a game's BGG average weight once for each recorded play. The game average counts each distinct played title once. The mode is a bucket, not the exact most frequent decimal rating.

BGG's collection numplays is the cumulative play count, not a dated play log. Results include only played games with known BGG weights, and missing weights never count as zero complexity.

Source: BGG XMLAPI2 Collection for play counts and statuses; Thing stats for averageweight. Thing requests: 20 IDs per batch, up to 100 parallel workers, with retries and local IndexedDB caching. BGG may throttle high concurrency.
