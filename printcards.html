Maker 3 Workflow

Step 1: Click Print and choose "Save as PDF".

Step 2: In Design Space, upload the PDF as "Print Then Cut".

Step 3: Sizing is critical. Set the uploaded image width to 7.44 inches in Design Space to maintain the exact 1:1 scale of these cards.

Step 4: Place cardstock on Blue LightGrip mat. Select "Medium Cardstock" setting.
