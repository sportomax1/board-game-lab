How to Play

BGG Game Speller reveals your collection game name letter by letter — can you guess it before the end?

  • Enter your BGG username to load your owned games
  • Watch the tiles flip and reveal letters one by one
  • Pattern matches — games starting with the revealed letters so far
  • Complete games — names that match exactly at the current length
  • The reveal stops when only one game remains
  • Use Undo to go back a letter, or Restart to pick a new game

Tip: Add this to your home screen for quick access!
