Source / rights

Game metadata uses BGG XML API2. The Chrome extension reads Community Wiki pages using the browser where you are signed into BGG. It may briefly open and close an inactive tab but never switches away from this app. Cookies and credentials are not sent to Board Game Lab. BGG access restrictions still apply. Cached copies stay in your browser.
