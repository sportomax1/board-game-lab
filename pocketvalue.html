Original Face Value formula
BGG Rating × Longest Shortest × Middle × 1,000 = Face Value
Face area = shortest × middle dimensions; depth = longest dimension. Both use the selected units. All three BGG measurements are sorted first.
Higher scores rank first. Volume formulas reward space efficiency; Face Value rewards the longest dimension relative to the smaller two dimensions (face area). Choose centimeters or inches above; changing units rescales scores but does not change rankings. Measurements depend on the selected edition.
Video inspiration

Pocket Value's Face Value scoring concept was inspired by a YouTube video exploring board-game box proportions and value. This app is an independent implementation.
