✂️ Cricut Print & Cut Guide

Recommendations for Print-then-Cut with Cricut Maker 3 in Design Space.

  1. Print Then Cut area: Supports ~6.75″ × 9.25″. For full-page layouts, split into sheets or print and trim before cutting.
  2. Export / Bleed: Export each sheet as PNG/PDF at 300 DPI. Add 0.02–0.125″ bleed. Don't remove registration marks.
  3. Upload to Design Space: Upload and choose Print Then Cut. Place on 8.5″ × 11″ canvas then Make It.
  4. Mat Required: Print Then Cut always requires a mat. Use StandardGrip for sticker paper or cardstock.
  5. Recommended Materials:
    • Printable Sticker / Vinyl: Fine-Point Blade, Pressure: Default, Passes: 1
    • Light-Medium Cardstock (65–100 lb): Fine-Point Blade, Pressure: More, test first
    • Heavy Cardstock (110–130 lb): Fine-Point Blade, StrongGrip mat
    • Chipboard: Knife Blade, StrongGrip mat, increase passes as needed
  6. Calibration: Run Print-then-Cut calibration in Design Space if cuts are misaligned.
  7. Alignment: Leave 0.125–0.2″ safety margin to account for printer shift.
