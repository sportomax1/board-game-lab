LlamaIndex Hybrid Search combines BM25Retriever (sparse/keyword) with VectorIndexRetriever (dense/semantic) using a weighted blend controlled by alpha (α). α=1.0 is pure vector, α=0.0 is pure keyword. The final RRF (Reciprocal Rank Fusion) score merges both rankings.
