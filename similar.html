📊 Similarity Scoring System

Games are compared across all attributes. Each matching attribute adds 1 point. Here's how scoring works:

  • Year Published - +1 if exact year match
  • Min Players - +1 if exact match
  • Max Players - +1 if exact match
  • Playing Time - +1 if within 15 minutes
  • Min Playtime - +1 if within 10 minutes
  • Max Playtime - +1 if within 10 minutes
  • Min Age - +1 if within 2 years
  • Categories - +1 per matching category (max 5 points)
  • Mechanics - +1 per matching mechanic (max 5 points)
  • Designers - +1 per matching designer (max 2 points)
  • Publishers - +1 per matching publisher (max 2 points)
  • Artists - +1 per matching artist (max 2 points)
  • Game Types - +1 per matching type (max 3 points)

💡 Higher total scores indicate games with more shared characteristics. Filter and sort to discover new favorites!
